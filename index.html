The whole idea, in three moves
1

Pick a pivot

Choose any number from the group. It is the yardstick for everyone else. Our C code uses the last number.

2

Partition

Smaller numbers go to its left, bigger numbers to its right. The pivot now sits exactly where it belongs and never moves again.

3

Repeat on each side

The left group and the right group are smaller sorting jobs. Do the same thing in each. A group with one number is already done.
